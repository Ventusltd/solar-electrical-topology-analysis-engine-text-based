AUTHORITY BOUNDARY

The Python package owns the engineering authority. The browser is an editor and projection surface. Authority mode may display supplied values, coordinates, routes, evidence states and receipt hashes. It must not independently recompute authoritative route lengths, resistance, voltage drop, loss or equipment conclusions.

The command response, committed response bundle and local bridge response are required to be byte-identical for the reference case. Any divergence is a failing authority condition, not a harmless presentation difference.

The existing Playground calculations remain useful exploratory work, but they are visibly non-authoritative. Historical V6 to V10 workbenches remain reproducible references and are not silently promoted merely because their tests pass.

UNRESOLVED EVIDENCE

Completion of the slice does not mean completion of the engineering programme as a whole. Forty-seven equipment evidence items remain unresolved or candidate. In particular, input identifiers that resemble MPPT labels are routing fixture labels, not proof of actual equipment MPPT mapping. Internal inverter DC topology, shared-bus behaviour, reverse-current blocking and PCE backfeed current remain unknown.

The generic 4 mm² and 6 mm² metal-coated Class 5 conductor resistance records remain candidates because exact source revisions and independent verification are not yet encoded. Legacy ideal-bulk resistance calculations remain historical, lower-bound or assumed outputs rather than finished-conductor authority.

No universal loop-area compliance threshold, universal bifacial multiplier, universal string-cable current rule or unevidenced protection trigger may be introduced. Standards, EMC, lightning, wet/dry environmental networks, plant ingestion and fleet intelligence require separate controlled builds.
