## Logic requiring later verification

The mobile-workflow section should be checked against current primary documentation for GitHub Mobile, Working Copy, Termux, `proot`, the named remote-terminal bridge, Codespaces, Expo and any claimed encryption, buffer, sandbox or compatibility behaviour. A useful workflow option is not automatically a mandatory architecture.

The agent-standardisation section should be checked against official Linux Foundation and project sources. MCP, A2A, Goose, AGENTS.md and any Agentic AI Foundation relationship, stewardship claim, adoption number or measured productivity benefit must be recorded separately rather than treated as one established standard family without verification.

The DORA percentages should retain their study design, date and correlational caveat. Repository commit counts are observations, not proof that AI caused low delivery performance.

The cold-voltage arithmetic should retain the illustrative temperature boundary. The temperature-coefficient method is linear over the stated calculation; it should not be called an exponential increase unless an evidenced nonlinear model is actually used. Application of a module measuring tolerance as a fully correlated worst-case voltage uplift across a string remains a design-method question and must not be described as a definitive violation before the tolerance meaning and applicable method are established.

The bifacial-current section sums published short-circuit currents. The forty-ampere inverter figure is described as maximum PV input current, while sixty amperes is the stated short-circuit-current limit per MPPT. Comparison with forty amperes is therefore a screening comparison, not yet proof of clipping, thermal stress or equipment incompatibility. The corresponding maximum-power current, rating definitions and manufacturer application method must be established.

The claim that string fusing is absent, unnecessary or ineffective must remain unresolved until the internal parallel neighbourhood, PCE backfeed, external sources and applicable protection method are evidenced.

The ninety-six-nanofarad module capacitance figure is an assumed parallel-plate upper bound, not a measured installed value. The field photographs support the existence of coiled slack and proximity to structure; they do not alone quantify inductance, capacitance, wet-state coupling or transient response.

The cable-sizing, temperature, voltage-drop, earthing and insulation-monitoring section contains multiple standards propositions. In particular, any double application of a 1.25 factor, universal 1–3 per cent voltage-drop limit, continuous 120 °C cable claim, generic bonding rule or universal one-megohm threshold must be checked against the exact equipment, installation, jurisdiction and current standards edition before implementation.

The arc-fault section should be treated as a research programme, not current engine authority. Claims about leading fire causes, arc temperatures, detectability by conventional protection, UL 1699B and IEC 63027 timing or energy requirements, reset rules, spectral bands, DSP architecture, neural-network accuracy and mandatory AI-driven disruption require exact source attribution and scope qualification.
