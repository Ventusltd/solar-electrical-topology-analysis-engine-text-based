Now the substance, which is that today produced two findings of very different kinds and it is worth being clear about which is which.

The first is that the continuity doctrine earned its keep under adversarial conditions, and there is a transcript to prove it. A quantitative error — comparing a module's short-circuit current against an inverter's maximum operating input current, two different quantity kinds — entered a briefing document, was repeated by me in a Trueself entry, was correctly identified by a review footnote, and was then escalated by a fourth model into a fabricated physical consequence about current clipping and thermal stress that does not follow from the premise. Four models, four passes, and the error grew more confident at every hop until the apparatus caught it. Pairing like with like, the correct figures are two strings summing to 34.70, 36.44 and 38.18 amperes on maximum-power current across the published rear-gain bins, all beneath the forty-ampere operating limit, and 36.90, 38.74 and 40.60 amperes on short-circuit current, all far beneath the sixty-ampere short-circuit ceiling. There is no compatibility breach on either correctly matched pairing. What survives is the weaker and still useful claim that rear-side gain consumes real headroom and must be a first-class electrical input rather than an energy-yield footnote.

That episode is the strongest argument the project has for its own governance, and it should be preserved as durable law rather than as an anecdote. The law it establishes is narrow and implementable: every quantity carries its kind, and comparisons between incompatible kinds are refused by the code rather than caught by review. Had that existed this morning the error could not have been written down, let alone propagated three times.

Three further corrections belong in the record, all of them mine. I described a photographed nameplate agreeing with a datasheet as field-measured agreeing with manufacturer-declared. It is not. A nameplate is field-observed marking that corroborates equipment identity; it is not an electrical measurement, and collapsing the two is exactly the silent promotion the bounded-observation law forbids. The evidence model needs three states there, not two. I treated a datasheet's plus or minus three per cent measuring tolerance as though its application across thirty series modules were an entailment; whether that tolerance is correlated or partly random, and which method governs maximum string voltage, is an open design question that must be recorded as a named policy rather than settled in prose. And I asserted that string fusing is absent because two strings in parallel cannot approach the module's series-fuse rating; that is a plausible explanation stated as a cause, when the internal parallel neighbourhood and the backfeed path both remain unresolved.

The arithmetic that survives all of this is worth restating because it is the reason the model needs work. Thirty modules at 45.9 volts open-circuit give 1377 volts at standard conditions, and at an illustrative minus ten degree case the published minus 0.25 per cent per degree coefficient yields approximately 1497.5 volts against a 1500 volt ceiling — two and a half volts of margin, with lower power bins giving approximately 1491 and 1478. A module profile bound at project level cannot answer that question where bins differ between string groups, so profiles must bind at string-group level and the bin, revision, temperature case and tolerance policy must appear in the receipt. The two factory leads differ by seventy millimetres, accumulating to 2.1 metres across a string and 50.4 metres across twenty-four, before any field conductor exists. And the geometry that governs surge behaviour is coiled by hand with a cable tie after the design is finished, recorded in no schedule, which is why declared slack must become a first-class quantity that defaults to unresolved rather than to zero.

The second finding of the day is that I was wrong about the state of the product, and the correction matters more than the corrections above.

I said the project lacked a demonstration a stranger could open and understand quickly. It does not. The published V8 leapfrog page does exactly that, and it does it in the way that is hardest to fake. Two rows of identical modules, fixed in position, one wired sequentially and one leapfrogged, with the additional far-end return of 39.67 metres printed beside the picture that explains why it exists. Anyone who has stood on a site understands it before reading a word of theory. And it argues against itself: it refuses to convert a theoretical reduction into an available one unless the module-lead reach screen passes, and it was screening infeasible when observed. Its lead-evidence selector offers manufacturer standard, manufacturer custom, measured on site, assumed from photograph and unknown, which is the evidence doctrine rendered as an interface control rather than described in a capsule. Its Build 025 reconciliation reports field-installed, factory-fitted and total circuit conductor separately and states plainly that the factory path can increase by more than the field path decreases, so total conductor may rise while contractor cable falls. A tool built to sell an idea would print none of that.

So the position on capability is better than the continuity corpus suggests. The doctrine is not aspiration; it has already reached a screen and behaved correctly there. What is weak is not the engineering and not the demonstration. It is the ledger — the machine-readable statement of which document is current, which programme is active, and what the single next goal is. That is now the binding constraint on everything else.

The honest ratio for the day is several thousand words of governance prose, one broken link, a stale pointer, and no code change. The apparatus that exists to protect delivery consumed the delivery. That is defensible while first principles are being repaired and corrosive if it becomes the standing mode. The next unit of work should be small, executable and boring.

Accordingly the position on what happens next. Repair the broken capsule link. Reconcile the programme-state pointer to whichever document the owner designates and name exactly one active tranche. Then quantity-kind typing and a comparison guard, because they make today's error structurally impossible rather than merely known about. Then the datasheet fixture with hashes, so no arithmetic anywhere reads a number from prose. Then rear-gain bins, string-group profile binding, per-pole lead lengths and declared slack. Everything else — arc faults, electromagnetic behaviour, standards work, project ingestion, the editable Studio — remains a later programme and should not be started under cover of this one.

Reserved to the Product Owner and to the company, and not to any executing agent: which Trueself is current; the licence choice and confirmation of rights to release, without which the open mission remains an aspiration and no external party can safely adopt anything; whether the evidence trail runs as one ledger or as a capsule per unit; and the authorisation of any programme beyond the repair tranche.

The position in one line. The engine already demonstrates its own value on a public page and the doctrine already caught a real error propagating across four models; what is missing is not capability or proof but a current, machine-readable statement of where the project stands, and until that exists every document added to the corpus makes the reload harder rather than easier.
